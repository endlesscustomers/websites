One-sentence: IMPACT's signature handwritten (Kalam) annotation with an optional hand-drawn arrow, for hero step labels and playful call-outs.

```jsx
<Callout arrow="down-right" size="lg">1. Learn the System</Callout>
<Callout size="md" color="var(--color-brand)">made me a millionaire</Callout>
```

`arrow`: none | down | down-left | down-right | left | right | up. `size`: sm | md | lg | xl (Kalam scale). Use sparingly — one or two per view.
