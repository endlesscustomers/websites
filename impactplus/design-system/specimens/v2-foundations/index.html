01 — Typography

Strong headlines.
Comfortable reading.

General Sans carries the assertion. Proxima Nova carries the explanation. Two paragraph sizes create hierarchy without making pages feel assembled from unrelated parts.

Display / 800

Become the most trusted company in your market.

H1 / 800

A roadmap your whole team can act on.

H2 / 700

Know what to fix first.

H3 / 600

Clarity before implementation

Lead paragraph / 20–22px

You should not need a six-month engagement to understand what is holding your growth back. The lead paragraph introduces the central idea and establishes why the section matters.

Standard paragraph / 18px

Standard body copy explains the idea with enough detail to be useful. Paragraphs stay focused, use clear transitions, and avoid burying the answer beneath unnecessary setup.

  • Lead with the answer. Make the first sentence useful on its own.
  • Use bold selectively. Emphasize the takeaway, not half the paragraph.
  • Keep lists scannable. Four to seven points is the normal range.

This is a fit when…

  • The problem is a priority now.
  • Leaders are ready to act.
  • The team values candor.

Probably not when…

  • There is no urgency.
  • No one owns the work.
  • The goal is only validation.

Risk reversal

If we miss the mark, we make it right.

State the promise, the remedy, and the refund plainly. Put the assurance beside the decision—not in fine print.

Review the guarantee

FAQ with quick answer

What does the engagement include?

Quick answer: Name the scope in one useful sentence, then provide the evidence and detail a serious buyer needs.

What happens after the work?

Clarify ownership, optional next steps, and any future commitment.
