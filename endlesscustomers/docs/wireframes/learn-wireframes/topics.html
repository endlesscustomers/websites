1 Two paths, one component: type-ahead matches show parent context ("Selling Seven · in Video") so users always know where they are in the tree. Browsing shows 12 parents → subtopics.
2 Every row shows a content count. Topics with fewer than 2 items hide from this UI automatically (page still exists for SEO).

4 HubSpot mechanics: topics live in a HubDB table (label, slug, parent, featured, order). HubDB multilevel dynamic pages generate /learn/topics/parent/child URLs natively. Blog tags mirror subtopic slugs; capped at two levels by rule.
