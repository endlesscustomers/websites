## Proposed family structure (revised)

The manuscript made one thing obvious: the section should be organized by the book's own architecture, the 4 Pillars, plus three wrappers. This replaces the six families in core-concepts-strategy.md:

1. **The System** — foundations, why it works, the worldview
2. **Say** — content & The Big 5
3. **Show** — video & YouTube
4. **Sell** — sales, Assignment Selling & self-service
5. **Be More Human** — messaging, brand & AI
6. **The Program** — getting started, milestones & measurement
7. **Fit & Decision** — is this for me

Organizing by pillar reinforces the entities themselves: every concept page's "part of" marker points at a pillar, which teaches the system's shape with every visit.
