My honest take

  • Yes, go this way. It's the real Apple sub-nav pattern and it makes cross-page navigation a first-class action instead of a buried module.
  • The trigger shows progress, so the dropdown isn't hiding the journey. Dots + "Step 2 of 3" + title means you orient at a glance and navigate on click.
  • One bar, two clearly-separated jobs. The divider keeps "switch pages" and "scroll sections" from blurring.
  • It lets us drop the heavy bottom module. The bar carries the journey now; the bottom of each page just needs a simple "Next: Step 3 →" nudge.
  • One watch-out: on a phone the anchors collapse, so the switcher + CTA must stay. That's fine, the switcher is the more important of the two on mobile anyway.
