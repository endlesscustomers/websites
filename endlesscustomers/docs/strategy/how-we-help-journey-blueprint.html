How We Help — the three-step journey

The good news: this journey is already half-written in the copy. The audit page ends with "the choice of what's next" (run it yourself, focused projects, or go all-in), and the coaching hero already says "Your Path to Endless Customers starts here." The pages just aren't connected, and the middle step has no page. This blueprint threads them into one path before we build.

The connective device — one bar, on all three pages
A single Path bar replaces the three different in-page sub-navs. It shows where you are, lets you jump, and (paired with a "next step" handoff at the foot of each page) makes the 1 → 2 → 3 movement real. Shown here on Step 2.

Internal context (not page copy): the tell that a Step 2 client is ready for Step 3 is often the moment they hire a full-time content manager. Until then they're not staffing a full marketing team, which is exactly why the do-with-you, teach-as-we-go model fits this stage.

Pricing — re-analyzed to mirror the three steps

You're right that pricing should go hand in hand with the steps. So the Pricing page stops being "coaching pricing" and becomes the money story for the whole journey, in the same 1·2·3 order, carrying the same Path bar:

  • The Audit — a defined, published price. The clearest, lowest-commitment number.
  • Get Started work — scoped per project, with honest ranges. Do-with-you, so the price reflects what you take on versus what we do.
  • The Coaching Program — the model you already have ($3,500 base + 90-Day Cycles), as the destination.

One standalone page at /how-we-help/pricing (old URL 301'd), both old sub-navs stripped. Each step page mentions its own cost at a high level and links here.

What makes it feel like one journey
  • One shared primary CTA, "Schedule a Call," on every page (and pricing) — the single conversion action all three drive toward, while the Path bar handles movement between steps.
  • The Path bar on all three pages (and pricing) — consistent orientation, replaces three mismatched sub-navs.
  • A forward handoff at the foot of every page, so each one ends by pointing at the next. Step 1 already has the raw material for this.
  • An entry ramp before Step 1 — the Free AI Diagnostic is the zero-commitment way in, then the audit is the real Step 1.
  • Honest back-doors — someone landing on Step 3 who isn't ready can step back to the audit; a Step 2 client who's flying can jump to coaching. The path guides without trapping.
