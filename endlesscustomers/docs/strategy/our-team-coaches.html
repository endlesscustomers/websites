How I read your notes, and what each became

  • Two doors, not a numbered path. How We Help's left is a 1-2-3 journey for one audience. Here the two left items are parallel doors for opposite audiences (a prospect choosing a coach, and talent wanting to join), so they're equal-weight cards with no numbers and no implied sequence.
  • The coach is the quarterback. Door one leads with your GP-and-specialist model: one coach owns the journey, specialists come in for specific work. That sells a real advantage over a solo consultant or a faceless agency, right in the nav.
  • "Become a Coach or Advisor," your wording. Specialists still get a track on the page, but the label leads with coach and advisor since those are the priority recruits. The contractor framing (Vistage Chairs, Petra coaches) is set in the sub-copy.
  • Advisor is flagged "Recruiting Now." Since business development is the heavy current focus, the advisor track carries the only badge in the menu, the same dd-badge-new component used live elsewhere.
  • The advisor owns the first conversation, not a grid card. Rather than tagging advisors as "advisor" in the coaches directory (confusing for a prospect), they surface at the conversion moment: the bottom strip concierge here, and the intro-call CTA on the page. Their visibility goes up, the directory stays clean.
  • The full team keeps its own home. "Meet the Full Team" leads the Company cluster on the right, the existing /team/meet-the-team repurposed as the complete roster (leadership, specialists, marketing & ops, everyone) with its role tabs intact. The new /coaches door is the focused, bookable prospect cut of that roster. Leadership and back-office credibility content sits where it belongs, with the company info, not on the buying door.
  • Right side is the supporting "Company" cluster. Meet the Full Team, Our Story / Vision & Values, Awards, and Careers. Careers is staff jobs (Workable) and is deliberately separate from the contractor recruiting door on the left.

Open decisions for you. (1) What we call the contractors in public copy. Vistage has "Chairs," EOS has "Implementers." Are yours "Certified Endless Customers Coaches / Advisors," or something shorter? It sets the tone on both doors. (2) URLs. Proposed: /coaches for Meet the Coaches & Specialists, /team/meet-the-team stays as the full-team roster, /team/become-a-coach for the recruiting page, and Careers stays at /team/join-the-team (or renamed to /careers). (3) Data prerequisite. Find-a-Coach needs real booking_url, specialties, location, and a new industries_served tag per coach. Today none of the nine have booking links. I can scaffold the fields and placeholders so we can build, then you collect real values before launch.
