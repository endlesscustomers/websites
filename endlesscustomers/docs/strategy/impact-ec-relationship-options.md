# IMPACT and Endless Customers: The Relationship Decision

**Status:** DECIDED by Bob, June 9 2026. Direction confirmed: **everything lives under the Endless Customers brand; IMPACT is only the company that owns it.** This is Model A executed with Model C's discipline. The done-with-you / done-for-you distinction is real and useful, and all services are stages of one Endless Customers journey (audit/diagnostic on-ramp for the not-yet-ready, services that fix what's broken and earn quick wins, the coaching program as the destination, add-on services alongside coaching). The site must serve three entry points at once: the unaware ("I have a customer problem, don't know where to start"), the ready ("I've been doing this, I want the best coaching"), and existing clients ("I want a specific add-on service now"). How We Help is being restructured to reflect that order of operations while still letting people jump straight to a service. Brief kept for rationale and the email/signature follow-through.

**Why this exists:** Tom's review surfaced the one question that gates the rest of the site. Until we name how IMPACT and Endless Customers relate, "How We Help" can't be finalized, because nobody can say whether that section means "how IMPACT helps" or "how Endless Customers helps." Everything downstream (the services menu, the email addresses, the signatures, the footer, the EOS-style coach idea) flows from this single decision.

---

## The actual question

Two sub-questions, in order. Answer the first and the second gets easier.

1. **Is Endless Customers the master brand, or the methodology brand?**
   - *Master brand:* Endless Customers is the company the customer experiences. IMPACT is the parent behind it. Everything (coaching, services, book, academy, conference, team, email) rolls up under Endless Customers.
   - *Methodology brand:* Endless Customers is the system and the movement (the book, the framework, the certification, the community). IMPACT, or certified partners, is who you hire to implement it.

2. **What is this website's single job, and who do we hand the visitor to at the end?**
   This is Tom's real concern. A visitor who finds Endless Customers and then talks to "somebody from IMPACT" should never feel a switch happened. The site's job and the handoff have to be one continuous story.

---

## Three models

### Model A. Endless Customers as master brand (your stated vision)

Endless Customers becomes the brand for everything. IMPACT becomes the parent company, disclosed for transparency and for the people who already know the IMPACT name. Emails move to @endlesscustomers.com. Signatures and the footer carry a single connective line: "Endless Customers is a brand of IMPACT."

- **Site's job:** Be the complete home of the Endless Customers brand. Teach the system, prove it works, and offer the ways to get help, all in one voice.
- **What happens to "Additional Services":** They stay on the site, but they are no longer a separate company's capabilities deck. They are Endless Customers' done-with-you and done-for-you implementation options. Tom's overwhelm critique still applies, so they get consolidated behind one clearly framed door (see "the services fix" below) rather than living as eight co-equal nav items.
- **Bait-and-switch risk:** Eliminated, because the brand the visitor met is the brand that helps them and the brand that emails them.
- **Cost:** Highest operational lift. This is a real rebrand: email, signatures, contracts, and the public story all change. The website is the easy part.

### Model B. Endless Customers as methodology, IMPACT as implementer

Endless Customers is the system, the book, the certification, the community, the conference. IMPACT (and eventually certified partners or coaches) is who you hire to do it. This is the EOS model you pointed to: EOS owns the methodology and the books; EOS Implementers and partners deliver it.

- **Site's job:** Teach the system and show, at a high level, what implementation looks and feels like. Then route the visitor to a solution: IMPACT, a certified coach, or a partner.
- **What happens to "Additional Services":** They move off this site to impactplus.com (or a partner directory). This site never claims to be the agency, so there is nothing to switch away from.
- **Bait-and-switch risk:** Lowest, but only if the handoff is explicit ("Implementation is delivered by IMPACT and our certified partners").
- **Cost:** Lower brand lift, but it splits your story across two sites and arguably caps how big the Endless Customers brand can get. It also runs counter to your stated vision of moving everything to EC.
- **Bonus:** The EOS-style "Become a Coach / Find a Coach" idea fits this model natively.

### Model C. Endless Customers as master brand, with a disciplined two-path site (recommended blend)

Take your master-brand vision from Model A, and adopt Tom's discipline about the site's job from Model B. Endless Customers is the brand. The site leads with the system and what implementation looks like, then offers two clear, equal-weight paths:

- **Learn and implement it yourself:** the book, the academy, the free Learning Center.
- **Have us implement it with you:** coaching plus a consolidated set of implementation services, delivered by the IMPACT team operating as Endless Customers.

IMPACT is disclosed as the parent for transparency. The services stop reading as a distracting menu and start reading as "here's what getting help looks like."

- **Why this is the recommendation:** It keeps your vision (everything rolls up to Endless Customers) while fixing exactly what Tom flagged (the site currently feels like an agency capabilities deck bolted onto a brand site). It gives the visitor one obvious next action instead of eight.

---

## My recommendation

Go with **Model C**: Endless Customers as the master brand, IMPACT as the disclosed parent, and a site whose job is to teach the system and present two clean paths to implementing it. It honors your direction without inheriting the confusion Tom called out. The work then becomes mostly editorial and structural, not a strategic fork.

This is a positioning call, not a design call, so it's yours and Tom's to make. The brief is meant to make that conversation short.

## What each model changes on the site

### The "How We Help" fix (applies to A and C)

The dropdown today carries a featured audit, a coaching column, a proof column, and eight "Additional Services" across two columns. The eight services are the source of Tom's "this feels like IMPACT, not Endless Customers" reaction. The fix in both A and C:

- Lead with the system and what implementation looks like, not a service grid.
- Collapse the eight services into **one** "Services" or "Done With You / Done For You" destination, framed as how Endless Customers helps teams implement. One nav item, not eight.
- Keep coaching and the proof column. They belong.
- Result: the dropdown goes from a capabilities deck to a focused "here's how you get this done" story.

In **Model B**, the services leave the site entirely and the dropdown becomes "Get Help Implementing," which routes out to IMPACT or partners.

### The branding fix (independent of the model, do it regardless)

Both you and Tom want more of the book's black and less wall-to-wall blue. The codebase confirms the imbalance: the palette is described in its own comments as a "cool-gray palette" with blue wired in as the semantic accent that cascades everywhere, while pure black is currently restricted to bold emphasis only. The fix is to re-rank the palette, not remove the blue: anchor surfaces and large display type in the book's near-black, and let blue do true accent work on lines, links, and key buttons. A side-by-side comparison is built so you and Tom can see it before anything propagates.

### The email and signature story (applies to A and C)

Moving to @endlesscustomers.com only works if the connective tissue is consistent everywhere: email signatures, the site footer, and the about page all carry one line that says Endless Customers is a brand of IMPACT. That single sentence is what prevents the "wait, who is IMPACT?" moment for people who knew the old name. Worth drafting that canonical sentence once and using it in all three places.

### The EOS-style coach idea

"Become a Coach" and "Find a Coach" are strong, and they're easiest to justify under a model where Endless Customers clearly owns the coaching relationship (A or C natively, B if you build a certified-coach layer). Parked as a TODO item tied to this decision rather than designed now.

## Decision checklist for the Tom conversation

1. Master brand or methodology brand? (Sub-question 1)
2. In one sentence, what is this website's job?
3. At the end of that job, who do we hand the visitor to, and how do we name them so it never feels like a switch?
4. Do the eight services stay (consolidated) or leave (to IMPACT/partners)?
5. Are we committing to @endlesscustomers.com email, and what is the one canonical "brand of IMPACT" sentence?

Answer these five and the How We Help section, the services, and the email migration all resolve at once.
