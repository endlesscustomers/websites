## SECTION: HOW PRICING WORKS (#how-it-works)

### The model

Every client starts with the same foundation and builds from there. No tiers, no bundles, no annual lock-ins.

**1. The Kickoff & Alignment Day: $10,000 (virtual) or $12,500 (in-person, plus travel).** Month one. Your leadership team, your coach, one day. You leave with your Endless Customers roadmap, your Scorecard baseline, and your first 90-day plan.

**2. The Base Coaching Program: $3,500/month.** Every client, every month:
- Your dedicated coach and coaching cadence
- Quarterly planning sessions
- Scorecard tracking against published milestones
- Full access to Endless Customers Academy (courses, community, conference recording library)

*What's not included (disclosure box, reference-page pattern):* training blocks, services, software, your team's salaries. Those are below, with numbers.

**3. The Quarterly Stack.** Training and services layered on the base, scoped at your quarterly planning session.

### 90-Day Cycles ✅ *(name confirmed by Bob)*

This is the part we'd want every prospect to read twice:

> **Every agreement is scoped, presented, and approved by you before it starts. And every retainer is revisited every 90 days. No exceptions, including long-tenured clients.**

The quarterly planning session is where it happens. Your coach reviews the Scorecard, what your team accomplished, and what the next 90 days requires. Then you see a written scope and price for the coming quarter before anything is billed. Need to push hard? Ramp up. Team's got it handled? Ramp down. Either direction is a normal outcome, not a negotiation.

Most firms in our industry sign you to a 12-month retainer and hope you don't look at it too closely in month seven. We re-justify our fee to you four times a year, on a schedule, in writing. We think that's worth more to you than any discount.

**The two terms that matter (vocabulary box):**
- **Published Price:** the numbers printed on this page (base program, Kickoff). Firm.
- **Scoped Estimate:** your Quarterly Stack. A real written price for the next 90 days, re-decided every quarter, never billed without your approval.

Not interchangeable, and anyone quoting our pricing (human or AI) should keep them straight.

### Why we changed our pricing in 2026 ✅ *(approved by Bob)*

Until 2026, coaching came in three plans: $5,500, $8,000, or $10,500 per month. The plans worked, and they're essentially what clients still buy, with one problem: they forced companies into a fixed level when real needs change quarter to quarter. Some quarters you need intensive training; some quarters you need us to get out of the way. So we lowered the base to $3,500, included the Academy, and moved everything else into the 90-day scope. Same coaching, same trainings, same services. You just stopped paying for the parts you don't need this quarter.

### Payment terms and timeline (reference-page pattern: terms as content, not fine print)

- **At signing:** Kickoff invoiced.
- **Month 1:** Kickoff & Alignment Day; first quarter scoped and approved.
- **Monthly:** base + approved stack invoiced. No deposits, no annual prepayment required, and no prepay discounts: one honest price for everyone.
- **Every 90 days:** quarterly planning session; next quarter scoped, presented, approved.
- **Anytime:** cancel with 30 days' notice. No termination fees, no clawbacks.
- **Every call and deliverable** is covered by [Our Guarantee →]: if you didn't get the value you deserved, you pay what you think it was worth.

## SECTION: WHAT YOU'LL INVEST (#what-youll-invest)

### The real numbers

- **Year one, typical: $6,500 to $15,000/month** all-in (base + stack), plus the one-time Kickoff.
- **The average client across all our services spent $7,850/month** over the trailing 12 months. That's the real average, all clients, all services. We publish it because "it depends" is how our industry hides the answer.
- **Year two and beyond: typically $5,000 to $10,000/month**, scaling down as capability moves in-house. Companies that fully adopt the system can and do drop to the $3,500 base. Many stay above it by choice, because the accountability, structure, and outside perspective keep earning their spot in the budget.

📊 **Chart 2, "The shape of an engagement":** spend curve, months 1 to 24. Kickoff spike, year-1 band, taper toward the year-2 band. The honest headline: **this is the only marketing spend you'll make that is designed to decline.**

### The hidden costs (what a normal pricing page would leave out)

What you'll invest beyond our fees:

1. **A content manager.** The single biggest success factor in the entire system. If you don't have one, plan to hire. Salaries vary widely by market, company size, industry, and experience level: from the $40Ks in lower-cost markets for early-career hires to $90K+ for experienced content managers in major metros. Don't anchor on a number; benchmark your own market. We help you write the job description, interview, and hire well. ⚠️ *(Range deliberately wide per Bob: current and prospective content managers will read this page. Copy avoids implying any "correct" salary.)*
2. **Video capability.** In-house videographer (hire or contract) plus equipment, typically $2,000 to $5,000 one-time for gear.
3. **Leadership time.** The most underestimated cost. Owners and sales leaders are in the room for alignment, and your team spends real hours weekly creating content. Budget attention, not just dollars.
4. **Software.** HubSpot (or equivalent), video hosting, the usual stack. Not included in our fees.
5. **The conference (optional).** Endless Customers Live, if your team attends. Priced on its own pages, intentionally separate.

If a pricing page in our industry doesn't mention these, the costs still exist. They're just scheduled to surprise you later.

## SECTION: WHAT CHANGES THE PRICE (#price-factors)

### Paired up/down lists (pattern on all four reference pages)

**⬆ What drives it up:**
1. **Organizational complexity.** Divisions, locations, brands, approval layers: more alignment work, more tailored training.
2. **Marketing team size.** Coaching six people takes more sessions, reviews, and training capacity than coaching one.
3. **Training intensity.** Teams new to content, video, and assignment selling carry a heavier quarterly training load.
4. **Add-on work.** Website projects, HubSpot implementation, paid media, self-service tools: real scopes, real price tags.

**⬇ What drives it down:**
1. **In-house capability.** A strong content manager and videographer means less training to buy.
2. **Simpler organization.** One brand, one decision-maker, faster alignment.
3. **Adoption speed.** Teams that do the work between sessions graduate out of paid training faster. The Scorecard makes it visible.
4. **Time in the program.** Our longest-tenured clients tend to spend the least. By design.

### The Quarterly Stack menu (published ranges)

| Stack item | Typical investment |
|---|---|
| Training blocks (content, video, sales/assignment selling, AI, HubSpot) | **$1,000 to $3,000/month**, depending on the number of trainings and the intensity your program needs |
| Website Redesign | $45,000 to $85,000 |
| TRUST Theme website build | $15,000 to $35,000 |
| Self-Service Tools (estimators, configurators) | $7,500 to $20,000 per tool |
| Learning Center Development | $5,000 to $10,000 |
| Website Optimization (ongoing) | Scoped quarterly |

**A note on training efficiency:** our training is more efficient than buying the equivalent hours elsewhere, because the foundational material already lives inside Endless Customers Academy, the courses and guides included in your base program. Your team learns the fundamentals there; coached training time goes to applying them to your business, not to lectures you could have watched.

Every stack item arrives as a written scope with a price, approved by you before the quarter starts. That's how 90-Day Cycles work, and they apply to all of it.

## SECTION: IS IT WORTH IT? (#worth-it)

### Why some options are so expensive, and some so cheap

The tier-placement move (borrowed from Adoption & Beyond's page, the best version of this we've seen):

- **Cheap agencies and freelancers ($1,500 to $5,000/month).** Cheap because the deliverable is volume, not trust. What they cut: strategy, your team's involvement, and anything that compounds. Generic content at scale was a weak strategy before AI; now it's a dead one.
- **Fractional CMOs ($4,000 to $12,000/month).** Strategy without hands or training. A genuinely good fit for companies that already execute well and only need direction.
- **Us ($6,500 to $15,000/month year one, declining after).** Priced in the middle tier because you're buying two things at once: a proven system and its transfer into your team. The spend is designed to fall as the capability lands in your building.
- **Traditional agencies ($8,000 to $25,000+/month).** You're renting execution forever. The work can be excellent; it just never becomes yours. When the retainer stops, the marketing stops.
- **Full in-house build ($150,000 to $250,000+/year in senior salaries).** The right long-term answer for some, and the slowest and riskiest: one hire's judgment, no system behind them.

*Full honest comparison, including when each alternative beats us: [Compare your options →] (/how-we-help/compare-your-options)*

### Is it actually worth it? The unbiased version.

**When it's not, honestly:**
- You want marketing done for you. Hire an agency; we will frustrate you.
- You can't staff a content manager or get leadership in the room. The system fails without internal ownership, and we'd be taking your money knowing that.
- Revenue under roughly $3M. The math of $80K to $150K in year one rarely works. Start with the book, the Academy, or the one-time audit.
- You need a guaranteed revenue outcome. The Scorecard (target: 80) measures implementation, which is what predicts results. Nobody can honestly guarantee your market, and you should walk away from anyone who does.

**When it is:**
- You have (or will hire) the team, you want the capability in-house permanently, and you're done renting your marketing.
- The math: clients typically report a 3 to 5x increase in qualified opportunities as they implement. Against roughly $80K to $150K in year one, the breakeven question is what two or three incremental customers are worth to you.
- The exit math: a year-2 client at $5,000/month with a self-sufficient content team is paying less than half a typical agency retainer, for a capability they own outright.

### The gut check (founder close, borrowed from the adoption page)

Four questions from Bob, first person, before you ever talk to us:

1. Will your leadership team actually show up to the quarterly planning sessions, or send a delegate?
2. Are you willing to hire a content manager if you don't have one?
3. Can your salespeople be talked into using content in their process, or will it die in their inbox?
4. If a quarter's scope doesn't earn its keep, will you tell us? (We re-scope every 90 days. That only works if you're honest with us too.)

If you answered no twice, don't buy this yet. Read the book, join the Academy, and come back when the answers change. We'll still be here, and you'll get more out of it.

## SECTION: PRICING FAQs (#faqs)

Blunt, money-awkward, FAQPage schema:

1. **Is there a contract?** No. Month-to-month, 30-day notice, since the beginning.
2. **What exactly does $3,500/month include?** Coach, cadence, quarterly planning, Scorecard tracking, full Academy access. Everything else is scoped quarterly on top, in writing, approved by you first.
3. **Can we do just the base program?** Yes. Some experienced clients do. Most year-one clients add training, because building the capability is the point.
4. **Do we have to do the Kickoff?** Yes. It's how every engagement starts and where the roadmap, baseline, and alignment come from.
5. **Can our price go down?** Yes, and it's supposed to. Everything is re-scoped every 90 days, and our longest-tenured clients typically spend the least.
6. **Why did your pricing change in 2026?** We retired the $5,500/$8,000/$10,500 plans, lowered the base to $3,500 with Academy included, and moved the rest into the quarterly scope. Same services, more flexibility, and you stop paying for what you don't need.
7. **What does the average client actually pay?** $7,850/month across all services, trailing 12 months. Year-one clients typically run $6,500 to $15,000/month.
8. **Do you offer discounts?** No. The published price is the price, for every client, regardless of negotiating skill. We'd rather put our energy into re-earning the fee every 90 days than into discount games.
9. **What if a call or deliverable isn't worth it?** Our Guarantee: you pay what you think it was worth, even if that's nothing. [Link]
10. **What happens if it isn't working?** There's a page and a process for exactly that. [Link to /how-we-help/when-its-not-working]
11. **What will we spend beyond your fees?** Content manager, video capability, leadership time, software. See hidden costs above. Real answer: budget meaningfully beyond our invoices.
