# Endless Customers Academy — Platform Audit (IMPACT+ on Circle)

Reference doc for building the Endless Customers Academy landing page. Compiled from a walkthrough of the live IMPACT+ community (community.impactplus.com) on June 6, 2026, plus Bob's pricing/packaging screenshot.

## What it is

IMPACT+ (rebranding to Endless Customers Academy) is IMPACT's membership platform built on Circle. It combines an active practitioner community, the full library of courses and guides used with coaching clients, recordings from every live event back to 2018, planning tools, and gamified weekly challenges. 8,196 members total. Positioning inside the platform: "the premier hub for Endless Customers practitioners" for people "committed to becoming the most trusted voice in your industry."

## Core pillars (what membership includes)

### 1. Community
Active spaces: Introduce Yourself (63 posts), News & Announcements, Share Your Wins, Ask the Community, Small Group Discussions, Free Events. Real member activity is recent and steady (multiple posts per week from practitioners: interior designers, content managers, decking specialists, insurance, trucking, etc.). A separate gated section exists for Certified Coaches & Partners (directory, chat, referrals, coaching resources, monthly sessions).

### 2. Guides & Courses (the learning library)
35 total per the pricing table. Filterable by Free / Pro / Courses / Guides. Two generations of content:

New EC-branded Pro guides (17, the practical playbooks):
- What to Expect When Your Company is Kicking Off Endless Customers (guide + course)
- Preparing Your Team for Endless Customers & Alignment Day
- Building Your Content Foundation
- Your Role as a Content Manager / How to Hire & Onboard a Content Manager / Supporting Your Content Manager's First 90 Days
- Your Role as a Videographer / How to Hire and Onboard a Videographer
- Getting Started with YouTube and Social Media
- Implement / Mastering Assignment Selling, Powering the Assignment Selling Engine
- Building and Optimizing Your Sales Process
- Preparing & Optimizing Your Website for Endless Customers, Fundamentals of CRO and Website Optimization
- Setting Up Your Baseline Analytics & Tracking
- Sustaining Endless Customers Success

Courses (incl. legacy TAYA catalog): Masterclass: The Fundamentals of Endless Customers (led by Marcus Sheridan), Getting Started with AI, The TRUST Theme, Leading a Sales Team, How to Achieve and Maintain Company Buy-In, Using and Managing Your HubSpot Portal, Investing in Incredible Sales and Marketing Results, plus ~25 TAYA-era courses (Assignment Selling, Pillar Content, The Visual Sale, video, SEO, social, case studies, en Español, etc.).

Guide format is substantive: each walks through a complete process with assignments ("you'll get clear assignments as you go so you're actually doing the work"). Example outcome promise from Building Your Content Foundation: a mapped content strategy, a 90-day editorial calendar, a backlog of articles, first three articles live, and a repeatable weekly process.

### 3. Event Recordings
Every live event: Endless Customers Live 2026 Chicago, IMPACT Live 2025 Hartford, IMPACT Live 2025 Chicago, IMPACT Live 2024 Chicago, IMPACT Live 2023, 2022–2023 They Ask You Answer Summits, IMPACT Live 2019, IMPACT Live 2018. Plus free recordings (Fundamentals of Endless Customers, Episodic Content, One-to-One Video Email).

### 4. Endless Customers Tools (Business tiers)
- EC Game Plan: quarterly planning tool to set priorities
- EC Scorecard: progress/performance tracking
- EC Typical Journey™: 24-month roadmap of stages and milestones
The pricing table calls this the "Gameplan Tool" (Business/Business+ only).

### 5. Endless Missions + gamification
Bite-sized weekly challenges (categories: Brand Endorsement, Content Engagement, Social Amplification, Company Recognition) tied to a points system and leaderboard. Levels: Explorer (0) → Learner (10) → Contributor (50) → Connector (100) → Educator (225) → Pioneer (400) → Momentum Builder (640) → Trust Architect (820) → Market Leader (1,280).

### 6. Also in the platform
- IMPACT+ Library space: aggregates courses, upcoming events (webinars, workshops, live sessions), recordings, and Endless Customers Podcast episodes (lots of case-study episodes: Bill Ragan Roofing 3x revenue, Berry Insurance, Opes Partners 10x growth, MoveMobility +$6M, etc. — good landing-page proof material)
- Coaching AI Bot (per pricing table, all tiers) + Circle's built-in AI summarize
- Recommended Tech space
- Links out to: Get Coaching & Training, Certified Coach Directory, Leave a Review

## Landing page raw material

- Social proof: 8,196 members; named practitioner activity across many industries; case-study podcast titles with hard numbers
- Authority: Marcus Sheridan-led Masterclass; recordings back to 2018; the system behind the Endless Customers book
- Differentiators: these are the same playbooks IMPACT uses with paying coaching clients; do-the-work guides with assignments, not passive video; gamified accountability (missions, points, levels); whole-company access at Business tiers; conference passes baked into Pro+/Business+
- Tier value ladder: individual learner ($119) → individual + events ($350) → whole company + tools ($750) → whole company + events ($1,000)
- Naming note: platform still shows IMPACT+ branding throughout; rebrand to Endless Customers Academy is in progress. TAYA-era course names still in catalog.
