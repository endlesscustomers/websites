Why Paid, Done Right

Most ad spend buys clicks, not customers

Most ads shout for attention and hope the click turns into something. The best companies do the opposite. They run ads that amplify the trust-building content their audience already values, so paid media reinforces a relationship instead of interrupting one.

Swell connects your brand to a prospect's entire digital journey, across search and social, so the right message meets them wherever they are. Every channel works together, and every dollar is tied back to pipeline and revenue, not vanity metrics.

Amplify, Don't Interrupt

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Swell?
Swell is IMPACT's paid search and social company. We specialize in paid media strategy, copywriting, design, media buying, optimization, and reporting, adapting your best trust-building content into campaigns across a prospect's entire digital journey.
Which channels do you manage?
We run omni-channel campaigns across paid search (Google, Microsoft) and paid social (Meta, LinkedIn), plus retargeting and display and shopping and Amazon. The goal is to meet your buyer wherever they are in their digital journey.
Do I need a content or trust foundation before running ads?
It helps a lot. The best ads amplify content people already trust rather than shouting for attention. If you already have trust-building content, we turn it into paid campaigns; if you don't, we'll point you to the Endless Customers system to build it as we go.
How do you report on results?
We tie ad spend to pipeline and revenue, not vanity metrics. You get clear reporting that shows how campaigns are influencing real business outcomes, every dollar accounted for against pipeline.
Is paid media a retainer?
Yes. Paid advertising with Swell is an ongoing retainer engagement, because media buying, creative, and optimization compound over time. Scope and budget are set after we understand your goals and channels.
