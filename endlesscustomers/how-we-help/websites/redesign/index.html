FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a HubSpot CMS website migration cost?
Typically about $16,000 up to $63,000+, depending on the size and structure of your website and the scope of the work.
How long does it take to build a new website with IMPACT?
As a ballpark, expect a full redesign to take three to six months; a simpler refresh (aesthetic updates without major structural change) is faster. A straightforward HubSpot CMS migration can move in as little as 4–6 weeks.
Who am I going to talk to first?
Instead of a salesperson, our discovery process starts with an actual IMPACT Web Strategist who learns about your challenges and goals.
Will I be able to update the site myself after launch?
Yes. We build on flexible, easy-to-use HubSpot modules and train your team to update, test, and improve pages without ever touching code, so you own the site instead of renting an agency. Curious where you stand today? Run a free AI diagnostic.
Do you migrate our existing content and SEO, or start from scratch?
We migrate it cleanly. We map your content, set up redirects, and protect your rankings as we move, so you keep the SEO equity you've already earned rather than starting over.

Ready to move your site the right way?

Tell us about your site and goals. We'll map the fastest path to a clean HubSpot migration, and a team that can run the new site without code.
