Website Optimization & Training

Launching a website is only the beginning. This program gives your team the skills, coaching, and structured process to keep your site improving month after month: more qualified leads, better performance in search and AI platforms, and a website that works as a genuine sales asset for your business.

Overview

What Is Website Optimization and Why Does It Matter?

Website optimization is the ongoing process of improving a website's ability to attract the right visitors, convert them into sales opportunities, and support the buying process. It covers three disciplines:

Technical SEO & Content Optimization

On-page content, keywords, and site health that drive organic visibility.

Conversion Rate Optimization

Analytics and experiments that turn visitors into sales opportunities and pipeline.

AI-Powered Search Visibility

How your site performs in AI platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

Many companies treat their website as a one-time build. The companies that get the best results treat it as something they continuously improve. They review the data regularly, run experiments, fix what isn't working, and build on what is. That ongoing cycle is what separates a website that plateaus from one that keeps growing.

The challenge: running that cycle takes a specific set of skills most marketing teams were never formally taught. This program exists to close that gap.

Who It's For

Why Companies Come to IMPACT for Website Optimization

The companies we work with in this program fall into two groups.

Group One

Just launched a new website

Wants to start optimizing immediately rather than letting the site sit and plateau.

Group Two

Has a solid website, but no process for improvement

Has had a solid site for a while, but has never built a real process around improving it.

In both cases, what's missing is the same: a trained person on the team who knows how to read the data, run experiments, and keep the site improving.

When we ask what's holding things back, we hear the same things:

They're working with an agency on SEO or optimization, but they can't evaluate what's being done or connect the work to business outcomes.

The site isn't showing up in AI search tools like ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews, and nobody on the team knows how to change that.

Conversion problems on key pages have been sitting unaddressed because every fix requires a ticket or an agency call.

Real user data is being collected, but nobody knows how to read it or act on it.

Someone on the team has website management in their job description, but they've never had formal training on how to optimize a site or make data-informed decisions about it.

This program addresses all of that through structured, coaching-based training that builds real capability inside your team. The site improves as the training progresses. And your team finishes the engagement knowing how to keep improving it without outside help.

Two Ways to Get This Done

The IMPACT Training Model vs. the Full-Service Agency Model

The way we look at website optimization and coaching is different from most full-service marketing agencies. It's worth understanding the differences and the advantages of choosing one over the other.

A training-based model works best when…

  • You have a dedicated person whose role includes owning the website, with the capacity for structured learning alongside their regular responsibilities.
  • You want to understand your data, run your own experiments, and reduce dependence on outside vendors.
  • You want a payoff that builds over time. Skills compound, the team gets more capable, and the cost of maintaining and improving the site decreases as the engagement progresses.

A full-service agency model works best when…

  • Your team is stretched thin and genuinely cannot commit four to six hours per week to implementation work.
  • You need significant improvement in the next 60 days for a specific reason, like a sales push, product launch, or rebrand.
  • Your organization doesn't have a dedicated marketing resource, so building in-house capability isn't realistic until that changes.

Neither model is inherently better. They serve different situations. The honest question is which one fits where your team is right now.

Fit Check

Is This Program Right for You?

We're a good fit if…

  • You just launched a new website and want to start building the optimization process immediately, rather than waiting for problems to surface.
  • You have a dedicated person on your team whose role includes owning the website, with protected time for this work.
  • Your site is built on HubSpot or WordPress (the platforms our strategists work in deeply).
  • You're willing to commit to biweekly calls and doing the implementation work between sessions.
  • You want your team to understand your data and make decisions from it, rather than receive a monthly summary.
  • You want your site to perform in AI search tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews alongside traditional search.

We're not a good fit if…

  • There is no dedicated internal resource available for this work.
  • Your site is built on a platform other than HubSpot or WordPress.
  • Your team's bandwidth is genuinely too limited to participate consistently right now.
  • Your site needs a significant rebuild before optimization training can be effective. In that case, a website redesign should come first.

If you're in a situation where a full-service model makes more sense right now, we'll tell you that on our initial call. We'd rather point you toward a path that actually fits than take on an engagement that isn't set up to succeed.

The Long-Term Math

The Case for Building This Capability In-House

For teams that are a good fit for the training model, there is a meaningful long-term financial argument for building this capability internally.

Companies that rely on ongoing agency engagements for website optimization often find themselves in the same position year after year: the work is happening, but the knowledge stays with the vendor. When the relationship ends, the improvements don't sustain themselves, and the team has to start over with a new provider.

You learn skills that don't stop at the end of a project

Skills developed during this program carry forward, into every page you build and every decision you make after.

Your work outlasts the engagement

The processes and dashboards you build don't disappear when a contract expires.

You stop depending on vendors

Your team knows when you genuinely need outside help and when you don't.

That's a different kind of return than what most marketing investments produce.

What's Included

What's Included Each Month

Every engagement follows a structured monthly rhythm, designed to build real capability through doing, not just learning. The format flexes based on what's needed. Some months might include two training calls in a row or two review sessions back-to-back. Your strategist adjusts the cadence to match where you are in the process.

Two Strategy Calls

Alternating between training sessions, where we introduce a concept and walk through the tools and reasoning, and review sessions, where we work through what your team implemented, troubleshoot issues, and move forward.

Between-Call Implementation Work

After each training call, your team applies what was covered directly to your own website. We review that work before the next call and provide detailed feedback. That's where the site actually improves.

Weekly Project Management

Your strategist is available between calls for questions and to keep work moving. You won't spend a week stuck on something a quick conversation could resolve.

Quarterly Planning Sessions

Every quarter, we step back. We review competitive performance, identify where the site stands in both traditional and AI search, and build a clear roadmap for the next 90 days.

AI Tools Exploration

AI is changing how websites are found and evaluated faster than any other factor in the market right now. Every engagement includes dedicated time to explore how new AI tools apply to your specific site, finding applications that move your performance forward.

What Drives Cost Up (and What Keeps It Down)

Site size and complexity

Larger sites with more pages, legacy technical debt, or multiple business units require more strategist time to audit, analyze, and work through systematically.

Team readiness

Teams that complete implementation work consistently move faster and get more from every dollar spent. When implementation stalls, progress stalls.

Breadth of scope

Some teams run the full curriculum, then move on to consultative coaching. Others add design and development work alongside the training. Either way, scope affects the total investment over time.

How to Evaluate Your Options

Whether you're comparing this program to a full-service agency or weighing it against handling optimization internally without coaching, here are the questions worth asking any provider you're considering, including us.

Questions about who does the work

  • Who will actually be working on my account day to day? A senior strategist or someone more junior?
  • Will I be able to see and understand what's being done each month, or will I receive a summary report?
  • If I end this engagement after six months, what will my team know how to do that they don't know today?

Questions about results and accountability

  • How do you define success in this engagement, and how will we measure it together?
  • What happens if we're not seeing meaningful improvement after 90 days?
  • Can you show me clients who have genuinely reduced their dependence on outside help as a result of working with you?

Questions about long-term cost

  • What does year two look like financially? Will I still be paying the same rate for work my team should be capable of handling by then?
  • Does your model make my team more capable over time, or does it require ongoing engagement to maintain results?

Questions about AI and where search is going

  • How are you helping clients show up in AI search tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews alongside traditional Google?
  • What does your team do to stay current on how AI is changing website discoverability, and how does that get applied to client work?

These questions apply to us, too. We'd rather you ask them on the initial call than sign up without a clear picture of what you're getting.

FAQs

A Few More Questions We Hear Often

How long does it take to see results from website optimization?
Most companies see quick wins addressed within the first month during the audit phase. Meaningful, measurable improvements in organic traffic, lead generation, or AI search visibility typically emerge within 90 days. Compounding results build over six to twelve months as your team becomes more capable and the work becomes more systematic.
What does a dedicated website owner actually need to do week to week in this program?
Your website owner should expect to spend four to six hours per week on implementation work between coaching calls. This includes applying what was covered in training sessions, running experiments, reviewing analytics, and completing assigned tasks. The coaching calls themselves are biweekly. Leadership involvement after kickoff is minimal.
Can a small in-house marketing team realistically manage website optimization without a developer?
Yes, and that's specifically what this program is built for. The training focuses on what a non-technical marketing professional can own independently: content optimization, analytics interpretation, conversion testing, and AI search visibility. Design and development work that falls outside that scope is scoped separately.
How is IMPACT's website optimization coaching different from hiring an SEO agency?
An SEO agency typically does the work for you and delivers a monthly report. IMPACT's model trains your team to do the work, understand the data, and make decisions independently. The distinction matters financially: when an agency engagement ends, the knowledge leaves with them. When this coaching engagement ends, your team keeps the skills.
What does it mean for a website to show up in AI search tools like ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews?
AI platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Claude now surface answers directly to users without requiring them to click through to a website. If your site isn't structured to be found and cited by these tools, you're invisible to a growing share of your buyers. This program includes dedicated work on AI discoverability alongside traditional SEO. Want a quick read on where you stand? Run a free AI diagnostic.
We already have a website that performs decently. Is there still value in this program?
Yes. Most companies with a solid-performing website have never built a systematic process for improving it. They're leaving measurable performance on the table in areas like conversion rate, AI search visibility, and lead quality. This program builds the process and the team capability to close that gap.
What happens after the coaching engagement ends?
That's the goal of the program: that it ends. By the time most teams move out of the curriculum phase and into consultative coaching, they're running experiments independently, interpreting their own data, and making confident decisions. The engagement is designed to build a capability that doesn't require ongoing outside support to sustain.
