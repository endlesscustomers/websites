How we collect and verify reviews

Trust is earned with transparency, so here is exactly how these reviews work:

  • Third-party sources only. Every review on this page was published on an independent platform — HubSpot's Solutions Directory, Clutch, Google, or Facebook — and links back to its source. We don't publish reviews you can't verify.
  • We don't filter out criticism. The few non-perfect marks are right there on Clutch: one client wished for fuller scoping up front, another wished a coach transition had been flagged earlier. We leave them up because they make the rest believable — and they made us better.
  • No incentives for ratings. We never pay for reviews or offer rewards in exchange for a specific score.
  • Results vary. Outcomes depend on the work a team puts in. We share what reviewers reported, not guarantees.

Common questions about IMPACT reviews

Is IMPACT a legitimate company?

Yes. IMPACT has been in business since 2009 and BBB Accredited with an A+ rating since 2011. It is a HubSpot Elite Solutions Partner — the highest tier — with a 5.0 rating from 327 reviews (99% five-star) on HubSpot's Solutions Directory, a 4.9 on Clutch, and 35 Google reviews. IMPACT has worked with 500+ companies worldwide and is headquartered at 125 Commerce Court, Suite 9, Cheshire, CT 06410.

How can I tell these reviews are real?

Every review links back to the third-party platform where it was published. Reviewers are named with companies and dates, many name their specific IMPACT coach, and the same reviewers appear consistently across multiple platforms — Andy Roe, Bethany Sousa, Dana Svilar, Ariana Martinez, and Mike Ritzema all left matching reviews on two or more platforms. Clutch additionally verifies most reviews through analyst phone interviews.

What results do clients report?

Verified reviewers report: 1,500 new leads per month (industry association), web traffic up 18% with leads nearly tripled (Superior Trucking Payroll Service), 55% organic traffic growth with 4x qualified pipeline and 7x revenue over three years (SVRN Enterprises), and a 10x traffic increase (Applango). Results vary by team and effort — see our success stories for full context.

What do the critical reviews say?

The few non-perfect marks are specific and constructive. One Clutch reviewer wished for a fuller project scope at the outset (the project still finished on time and on budget). Another wished a coach transition had been communicated earlier. We publish criticism rather than filtering it — and we've changed our onboarding because of it.

How much does IMPACT's coaching cost?

Our pricing is published openly — on our Coaching Program & Pricing page and on our Clutch profile. As one reviewer put it: "Not cheap, but the best usually are not the cheapest" — and Clutch clients rate our value-for-cost a perfect 5.0.

Where can I leave a review?

If you've worked with us, we'd be grateful for an honest review on HubSpot's Solutions Directory, Clutch, Google, or Facebook. Critical feedback included.
