What is the Endless Customers Coaching & Training Program?

Take control of your sales and marketing with a proven system based on the national bestselling book Endless Customers. This program gives your team the tools, training, and guidance to build an in-house marketing engine, align leadership, and unify sales and marketing.

We're with you every step of the way: helping you create content that builds trust, use tools like HubSpot and AI with confidence, and generate more qualified opportunities, faster. If you want to become the most known and trusted brand in your market, this program will show you how.

Built for companies between $3M and $100M in revenue

The Endless Customers Coaching & Training Program is built for companies that want to drive serious growth with a strategy their team can own. This program is a fit for you if:

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I do Endless Customers or hire a marketing agency?
If you're looking for a hands-off solution where someone else takes over your marketing, hiring an agency might be the better fit. The Endless Customers Coaching & Training Program is for companies that want to build real in-house capabilities. If your goal is to create content that reflects your team's unique perspective, voice, and values, it needs to come from within. That's what this program helps you do. If you've been burned by agencies before and know you're done outsourcing your brand voice, this is the stronger, more sustainable path forward.
Can we implement Endless Customers without coaching?

Yes, it's possible to implement Endless Customers on your own. The book lays out the core principles, and many of the tools and frameworks are accessible through Endless Customers Academy and other resources. That said, coaching provides structure, accountability, and expert insight to help you avoid common mistakes and move faster. For most companies, coaching increases the chances of long-term success and helps build the right habits from the start.

If you plan to self-implement, a smart first step is our Marketing Audit & Recommendations: a senior coach audits your entire digital presence and hands you a prioritized plan your team can execute on its own.

How much of our team's time is required to make this work?
To fully implement Endless Customers, you'll need dedicated full-time staff focused on content and video — at minimum a content manager and ideally a videographer. Leadership must also make this a top priority. A general time breakdown:
  • Quarterly Planning Sessions: ~3 hours per quarter
  • Coaching Sessions (Leadership): 1 hour, 1–2 times per month
  • Marketing Team Trainings: 30–60 minutes, typically weekly or biweekly
  • Sales Training: 30–60 minutes, once or twice a month
  • Ongoing Learning: courses in IMPACT+ between trainings
Can this work if we already have an agency or are using other vendors?
Yes. Many clients come into this program with existing agency relationships, whether for paid ads, website updates, or other initiatives. We work alongside your agency partners to ensure alignment, efficiency, and maximum impact — guiding the overall strategy so every dollar spent contributes to sustainable growth.
What if we don't have a content manager or videographer yet?
We can absolutely get started without those roles in place. Helping you find the right person is a core part of what we do together. While the role doesn't need to be filled on day one, we do need a commitment to hire these positions, often starting with the Content Manager, right when we begin. We then train and onboard your new hire so they know exactly how to produce the content your strategy needs.
What happens after we complete the program?
Progress is tracked using the Endless Customers Scorecard, with a target score of 80. Most companies reach this level of mastery within 24 months. Many continue coaching as their business grows, while others move to a less intensive program. We also recommend running a new Alignment Day each year and staying engaged by attending Endless Customers Live.
What if my business is below $3 million in revenue?
The $3 million mark is a guideline, not a hard cutoff. It's often the point where companies can comfortably staff a marketing team and invest in the tools and coaching required. If you're below $3 million but ready to invest, the program may still be a good fit. If coaching is a "not yet," you're not stuck: Endless Customers Academy is built for teams self-implementing the system, Endless Customers Live happens twice a year, and our one-time Marketing Audit & Recommendations gives you an expert-built plan you can execute at your own pace.
How much does Endless Customers Coaching & Training cost?
The base coaching program is $3,500 per month and includes Endless Customers Academy. On top of that, training and add-on services are scoped, presented, and approved in 90-Day Cycles based on what your next quarter needs. Typical year-one spend is $6,500 to $15,000 per month, and the average client spends $7,850 per month across all services. See the pricing page for the complete honest breakdown.
Do you work with companies outside the U.S.?
We do. We often work with companies across Canada, Europe, and Asia-Pacific, including Australia and New Zealand, and we've seen the principles of Endless Customers deliver the same strong results across every market. Buyers everywhere respond to clarity, transparency, and trust.
What does the process look like to get started?

The first step is to schedule an initial call to assess your company's needs and goals. We'll discuss where your company is today, where you want to be, and what challenges you need to solve. We'll also determine if now is the right time to implement Endless Customers and whether we're the right fit for your business.

If we both decide to move forward, we build your plan. This includes helping you understand the process, setting expectations for the first 90 days, and most importantly, meeting your coach. No one signs on unless there's a strong alignment between your team and your coach.

If everything makes sense and the partnership feels right, we finalize the agreement and get started. From there, we schedule Alignment Day, your first major milestone on the Path to Endless Customers.
