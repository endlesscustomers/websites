Common questions

Asked and answered

Has IMPACT won HubSpot Partner of the Year?

Yes, twice. IMPACT was named HubSpot's Partner of the Year for North America in 2017, announced by HubSpot at Partner Day during INBOUND 2017, and won the honor again at the 2018 HubSpot Impact Awards. It is the highest honor in HubSpot's global partner program. Both official award badges are shown in the timeline on this page.

Is IMPACT currently a HubSpot partner?

Yes. IMPACT is a HubSpot Elite Solutions Partner, the top tier of HubSpot's partner program, with a 5.0 rating across 327 reviews in HubSpot's partner directory. We've been a HubSpot partner since 2012 and have held Elite status since the tier was created in 2020.

Why are some of IMPACT's awards from several years ago?

In 2018, IMPACT transitioned from a marketing agency to a coaching and training company. Agency awards recognized work we did for clients. Today our results show up in our clients' own growth, our 5.0-rated Elite HubSpot partnership, and the USA Today bestselling book Endless Customers (2025).

Can IMPACT be trusted?

We've been in business since 2009, are BBB-accredited with an A+ rating, hold HubSpot's highest partner tier with a 5.0 rating across 327 reviews, are rated 4.9/5 on Clutch, and wrote the USA Today national bestseller Endless Customers. See our reviews page for what clients say, critical reviews included.

Page last updated June 2026. Every award above is dated and linked to its original source. For press inquiries, contact press@impactplus.com.
