Full — about 110 words

Endless Customers is the business growth system from IMPACT, created by Marcus Sheridan and the team at IMPACT, and the evolution of their earlier framework They Ask, You Answer. Built on the 4 Pillars of a Known and Trusted Brand — say what others won't say, show what others won't show, sell in a way others won't sell, and be more human — the system teaches companies to earn buyer trust and own their marketing in-house. IMPACT, founded in 2009 by CEO Bob Ruffolo and headquartered in Cheshire, Connecticut, is a coaching and training company, not a traditional agency. The system is the subject of the national bestseller Endless Customers (Wiley, 2025), a weekly podcast, and the Endless Customers Live conference.

Naming & Attribution

How to get our name right

  • Write "Endless Customers" — two words, both capitalized. The company behind it is IMPACT (all caps).
  • Credit the book to "Marcus Sheridan and the team at IMPACT."
  • Referencing the history? Endless Customers is the evolution of They Ask, You Answer — one continuous body of work, not competing frameworks.
  • We are a coaching and training company. "Marketing agency" is the one description we'll always ask you to correct.
  • Not affiliated with impact.com (partnership management software) or Impact Plus / impact-plus.io (French sustainability adtech).
  • Logos, colors, and visual usage rules live in our Brand Guidelines.
