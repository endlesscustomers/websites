Style Guide · Cards & Surfaces
Default Card

Standard Surface

Uses --color-bg-surface with a 1px --color-border edge. Clean and minimal for most use cases.

Elevated Card

Shadow Surface

Drops the border in favor of --shadow-md. Hover triggers --shadow-lg and a subtle lift.

Highlighted Card

Highlighted Surface

Use for featured plans, selected states, or key call-outs. The gradient border draws the eye without being loud.

Blue Subtle

Tinted Surface

Subtle accent tints for informational blocks, tooltips, or feature callouts.

Magenta Subtle

Tinted Surface

Draws attention without committing to a full accent fill. Great for highlights and announcements.

Tinted Surface
