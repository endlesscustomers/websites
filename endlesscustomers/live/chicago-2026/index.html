About This Event

When and where was Endless Customers Live 2026?
Endless Customers Live 2026 was held March 30 – April 1, 2026 at the Loews Chicago O'Hare Hotel, 5300 N River Rd, Rosemont, IL. It was the first event under the Endless Customers name — the conference ran as IMPACT Live from 2018 through 2025.
Where can I watch the recordings?
Three attendee-favorite sessions are free on this page. The complete session library is in the IMPACT+ community — select recordings are free for all members, and every recording is included with a Pro membership.
Who spoke at the event?
Marcus Sheridan led the main stage and AI Day. Sessions and workshops were led by Bob Ruffolo, Stephanie Baiocchi, Brian Casey, Jessica Palmeri, Mary Brown, Sarah Schreck, and Chandni Desai of IMPACT, with guest speakers Kyle Jepson, Keven Ellison, and Chris Brisson. See the full session-by-session recap.
Will the same content be covered at the next event?
The three-day format and the 4 Pillars framework stay consistent, but speakers, workshop topics, and the AI Day content are refreshed for every event. Many companies attend every year and bring new team members each time.
When is the next Endless Customers Live?
October 5–7, 2026 at the Hartford Marriott Downtown in Hartford, CT — then April 5–7, 2027 back in Chicago.
