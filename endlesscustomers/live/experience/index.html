What is Endless Customers Live?
Endless Customers Live is a three-day, single-track conference and the only in-person event built entirely around the Endless Customers system. It combines main-stage training led by Marcus Sheridan and the Endless Customers coaching team, hands-on workshops, an AI-focused final day, and networking with 350+ business leaders.
When and where is the next Endless Customers Live?
The next event is October 5–7, 2026 at the Hartford Marriott Downtown in Hartford, CT. The conference then returns to Chicago, IL on April 5–7, 2027.
How much does Endless Customers Live cost?
Hartford 2026 tickets are $1,299 for individuals and from $799 per person for teams of 8+. Chicago 2027 early-bird tickets are $999 for individuals and from $649 per person for teams through December 31, 2026.
Who should attend Endless Customers Live?
Business owners, CEOs, sales and marketing leaders, content creators, and videographers. Companies between $3M and $100M in revenue tend to see the strongest results — and teams who attend together get the most out of it.
Can I watch sessions if I can't attend?
Yes. Free sample sessions and the event teaser are on our What It's Like page, and the complete recording library from every event is included for Pro members of Endless Customers Academy.
How do I sponsor Endless Customers Live?
Sponsorships start at $4,000 and put your brand in front of 350+ attendees, 70% of whom are owners and executives. View the packages on our sponsor page or email events@impactplus.com.
