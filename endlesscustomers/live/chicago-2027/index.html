What Is Endless Customers Live?

A full day of training and inspiration, led by the experts

Endless Customers Live 2027 is a three-day, single-track conference held April 5–7, 2027 in downtown Chicago, IL (venue announced Fall 2026). Early-bird tickets are $999 for individuals and from $649 per person for teams through December 31, 2026, and the event is built for owners, sales, and marketing leaders at companies between $3M and $100M in revenue.

At its core is one powerful, full day of training and inspiration led by the experts who've put Endless Customers into action. You'll leave with the clarity, tools, and momentum to make it happen in your own organization.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

When will the venue be announced?
We expect to announce the downtown Chicago venue and discounted room block in Fall 2026, alongside the full agenda. Early-bird ticket holders will be notified first.
What happens if I buy early-bird tickets and can't attend?
You may request a refund up to 30 days before the event; we'll issue a full refund minus processing fees. Within 30 days, you may transfer your ticket to another attendee or receive a credit toward any IMPACT event within the next 12 months. Tickets are fully transferable at any time.
I attended Chicago 2026 (or I'm going to Hartford) — will this be new content?
Yes. The three-day format and focus on the Endless Customers system stay the same, but speakers, workshop topics, and the AI Day content are refreshed for each event. Many companies attend every year and bring new team members each time.
Are there group discounts?
Yes, early-bird team pricing starts at 3+ attendees. Choose the group ticket type at checkout. For groups of 10+ please email events@impactplus.com.
Do you offer discounts for non-profit companies?
Yes, we offer a 15% discount on tickets. Please email events@impactplus.com for more information.
Who is the event best suited for?
Endless Customers Live is designed for business owners, CEOs, sales and marketing leaders, content creators, and videographers. Companies between $3M and $100M in revenue tend to see the strongest results.
