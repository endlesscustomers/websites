FAQs

Sponsor Questions, Answered

Do sponsors need to register?
You can bring as many people as you'd like but every attendee, even sponsors, needs a ticket. Sponsorship packages include 1-4 tickets depending on the package purchased, and additional tickets are at a discounted cost.
Which events can we sponsor?
Sponsorships are available for both Hartford 2026 and Chicago 2027. Multi-event packages are available at a discount — ask us.
Can we run a demo or giveaway at our table?
Yes — demos, giveaways, and raffles are encouraged. We'll coordinate timing with breaks and networking blocks so you get maximum foot traffic.
When is the sponsorship deadline?
Sponsorships are first-come, first-served and typically sell out about 8 weeks before the event. Logo placement deadlines for printed materials are 6 weeks out.
How do we get started?
Review the prospectus, then email events@impactplus.com. We'll set up a quick call to match you with the right package.
