The book lays out the entire framework start to finish, built on the Four Pillars of a Known and Trusted Brand, the same system thousands of companies use to grow.

1

Say what others won't

The Big 5 topics that earn outsized trust and drive qualified buyers.

2

3

Sell how others won't

Self-service tools and Assignment Selling that close better-prepared buyers.

Be more human

How to use AI and technology to feel more human to buyers, not less.

What you'll learn

  • Earn trust by answering your buyers' toughest questions better than anyone in your industry.
  • Turn your website into a 24/7 salesperson that builds credibility and drives conversions.
  • Let buyers self-educate and qualify themselves before they ever talk to sales.
  • Use video to connect with buyers and shape their decisions.
  • Build a culture of transparency your competitors can't match, in any industry.

Questions about the book

Is there an audiobook?

Yes. The Endless Customers audiobook is narrated by Marcus Sheridan and available on Audible. You can find it in the available formats above.

Where can I buy Endless Customers?

The hardcover is available from Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Bookshop.org, and Books-A-Million. The ebook is on Kindle, and the audiobook is on Audible. See all formats and stores →

Can I order copies in bulk for my team?

Yes. Orders of 10 or more copies unlock volume pricing, with options for custom branding and author add-ons. Explore bulk orders →

How is Endless Customers different from They Ask, You Answer?

Endless Customers is the evolution of They Ask, You Answer. It updates the framework into the Four Pillars of a Known and Trusted Brand and folds in everything the team has learned since, including how to stay human while using AI. If you loved the first book, this is the current, complete system.

Who is this book for?

Business owners, CEOs, and sales and marketing leaders in any industry who want to become the most trusted brand in their market. The system works at any stage of growth.

How long is the book?

288 pages, published by Wiley in 2025 (ISBN 9781394282784).

Can I read part of it for free first?

Yes. The free preview edition includes select chapters and the Four Pillars. Get the free preview →
