FAQ

Common questions

What is the Endless Customers Academy?
The Academy (formerly IMPACT+) is the official membership platform for the Endless Customers System. It combines an active community of 8,000+ practitioners, the full library of courses and guides our coaches use with clients, recordings from every live event since 2018, planning tools, and weekly missions that keep your team moving.
How is the Academy different from coaching?
Same system, different level of support. The Academy is the self-paced path: you work through the playbooks on your own schedule with the community behind you. Coaching puts a dedicated coach in your corner with private training, reviews, and accountability. Many companies start in the Academy and move to coaching when they're ready to go faster. And if you're already a coaching client, you don't need to buy anything here — full Academy access is included in every coaching agreement.
Do I need to read the book first?
No. The Fundamentals of Endless Customers Masterclass, led by Marcus Sheridan, covers the core concepts of the system. The book is a helpful companion, but it's not a prerequisite.
What's the difference between Pro and Business plans?
Pro plans cover one person and include a portion of the course library. Business plans cover your entire company — everyone on your team gets a seat — and unlock all 35 courses, guides, and templates plus the Game Plan tool for quarterly planning and progress tracking. If you're implementing as a team, Business is built for you.
How do the all-inclusive conference passes work?
We host two Endless Customers Live conferences each year. Pro+ includes one all-inclusive pass to each (a $2,298 annual value); Business+ includes three passes to each (a $7,794 annual value). Both plans also get 25% off any additional tickets for your team.
We mainly come for the conferences — does membership make sense?
Often, yes. If you attend Endless Customers Live regularly, Pro+ covers a pass to both conferences every year (a $2,298 value) and Business+ covers three to each (a $7,794 value) — so membership absorbs most of what you'd spend on tickets anyway, and the course library, community, and recordings ride along. It's not cheaper than tickets alone, but for regular attendees it's usually the better buy.

Want a faster path?

If you'd rather have expert guidance and accountability, our coaching program puts a coach in your corner — and includes everything on this page, full Academy access and all.
