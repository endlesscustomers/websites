Become the most known, trusted, and recommended brand in your market.

The way customers choose who to buy from has changed. Endless Customers is the proven system that makes your business the one they find, trust, and choose, in search, in the sales conversation, and in the age of AI.

Why customer acquisition got harder

Your buyers changed how they buy. Most companies didn't change how they sell.

Today's buyers research, compare, and ask AI who to trust long before they call you. If you are not the clearest, most honest voice in those moments, you are invisible when the decision gets made.

Leads are down, and the ones you get convert worse.
Sales cycles drag, and buyers compete you on price.
You rent attention from ads and agencies instead of owning it.
You're invisible where buyers now look: AI, video, and reviews.
70%

of the buying decision is made before a buyer ever talks to your sales team.

The short answer

Endless Customers is a proven system that helps your business become the most known, trusted, and recommended brand in your market. It aligns your marketing, sales, and leadership around what modern buyers and AI both reward: honest, helpful answers to the questions that drive a purchase.

It is the evolution of They Ask, You Answer, rebuilt by Marcus Sheridan and the team at IMPACT for an AI-first world.

What changes when you run it

Stop chasing customers. Start attracting the right ones.

Endless Customers fixes the root cause of expensive, unpredictable growth: a lack of trust at the moment buyers decide.

Get Found and Recommended

Be the business buyers find in search, and the one AI tools recommend when someone asks who to trust.

Earn Trust Before You Talk

Openly answer the questions others dodge, so buyers arrive already believing you. This is the heart of The Big 5.

Attract Better-Fit Buyers

Honest content and self-service tools let the wrong prospects opt out and the right ones raise their hand.

Shorten the Sales Cycle

With Assignment Selling, buyers learn before the meeting, so your team closes informed, pre-sold prospects.

Own Your Growth

Build an asset your team controls instead of renting leads from ads and agencies that vanish when you stop paying.

Align Your Whole Company

Marketing, sales, and leadership work from one playbook, so effort stops leaking and results compound.

How it works

Known and trusted brands do four things their competitors won't.

Diagram

The Endless Customers system at a glance: four behaviors (Say, Show, Sell, Be More Human) arranged around a center labeled "The most known, trusted & recommended brand in your market." Clean, code-built, EC blue line-draw.

1

Say What Others Won't

Answer the questions buyers really care about, openly: cost, problems, comparisons, and more. We call these The Big 5.

2

Show What Others Won't

Use video and a media-company mindset to build trust and human connection faster than text ever could.

3

Sell How Others Won't

Give buyers self-service tools and let content teach first (Assignment Selling), so people arrive informed.

4

Be More Human

Use AI and technology to deepen the relationship, not replace it. Buyers feel they know you before you meet.

Who it's for

Built for businesses whose buyers do their homework.

We do our best work with companies, roughly $3M to $100M in revenue, whose leadership wants to own their growth instead of renting it.

A strong fit if

  • Buyers compare options before they buy.
  • You sell something considered, where trust matters.
  • Leadership is ready to commit and be transparent.
  • You want an owned, lasting asset, not a quick fix.

Not yet, if

  • You want a quick outsourced campaign.
  • Leadership won't engage in the work.
  • You need leads by Friday with nothing to change.
  • Trust isn't part of how customers choose you.

Not sure you're a fit? We'll tell you straight. See Who We Work With.

Common questions

What people ask before they start.

What is Endless Customers?
Endless Customers is a proven system that helps your business become the most known, trusted, and recommended brand in your market. It aligns marketing, sales, and leadership around honest answers to the questions that drive a purchase. See the 5 Components.
How is this different from a marketing agency?
An agency does the work for you and takes it with them when they leave. We coach your team to own the system, so the capability and the results stay in-house. Compare your options.
Is this just "They Ask, You Answer" with a new name?
No. It's the evolution: the same trust principle, expanded into a complete system across content, website, sales, technology, and culture, and rebuilt for an AI-first world. About the book.
Will it work for my industry and business?
It works best when your buyers research before they buy and trust drives the decision, across home services, manufacturing, professional services, healthcare, and more. See who we work with.
How does Endless Customers help with AI?
The honest content that wins buyers is exactly what AI cites and recommends. We help you get recommended by AI, publish AI-ready content, and adopt AI safely. See how we help with AI.
How long until I see results?
Quick wins show up in weeks, meaningful results around 90 days, and full mastery over 18 to 24 months as the system becomes how your company operates.
What does it cost and what's the ROI?
Companies typically see 3 to 5 times more qualified opportunities. Pricing is transparent and depends on your starting point. See pricing.
Do I need to read the book first?
No. The Core Concepts & Frameworks are free, and the book has a free preview. Start wherever you like. Get the book.
